## Usage

```tsx
<Heatmap
  data={[
    { date: "2026-11-13", count: 4 },
    { date: "2026-11-14", count: 9 },
  ]}
  weeks={26}
/>
```

## Props

- `data`: one entry per day, `{ date: "YYYY-MM-DD", count }`. Missing days count as 0.
- `weeks`: how many weeks to show. Defaults to `26`.
- `endDate`: the last day shown. Defaults to today.
- `formatLabel(count, date)`: the text for the tooltip and screen readers.
- `loading`: shows a pulsing grid. `empty`: shown when every day is 0.

## How the scale works

Empty days use `foreground` at 8%. The other days fall into four levels, at 30%, 55%, 80%, and 100% of `foreground`, split by the quartiles of your non-zero counts. One very busy day does not wash out the rest.

## Accessibility

- The grid is a table with `role="grid"`: seven rows, one cell per day, and one tab stop. Arrow keys move between days, and Home and End jump to the first and last week.
- Each day has a label such as "9 contributions on Nov 14, 2026". The visual tooltip is hidden from screen readers to avoid reading it twice.
- With reduced motion, there is no lens, wave, or pulse.
