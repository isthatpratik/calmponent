## Usage

```tsx
<Textarea maxLength={280} placeholder="What changed?" />
```

Set `maxLength` to show a counter. It turns amber at 90% of the limit and red at the limit. Use it inside `Field` for a label and errors.

<TextareaStates />

## Props

Every native textarea attribute, plus:

- `warnAt`: the share of `maxLength` where the counter turns amber. Defaults to `0.9`.

## Accessibility

- The visible counter is hidden from screen readers, because reading it on every key is noise. Instead, a live region announces 50, 20, and 10 characters left, and when the limit is reached.
- The textarea grows with its content in browsers that support `field-sizing`. In other browsers, it can be resized by hand.
