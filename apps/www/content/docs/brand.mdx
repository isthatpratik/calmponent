## Name

Write **Calmponent** as one word with a capital C. It joins "calm" and "component": quiet, intentional motion and a calm, finished look. Describe it as "an open-source React UI library for shadcn/ui and Tailwind CSS".

## Mark

The mark is a rounded square with a dot in the middle: a component and its status light. It sits on a 24-unit grid. The square is 18 units wide with a 5-unit radius and a 2-unit stroke, and the dot has a 2.5-unit radius.

<BrandMarks />

- Use the mark in one color only: the current text color, black, or white.
- Keep clear space of one dot diameter on every side.
- Do not add color, gradients, shadows, or outlines. Do not stretch or rotate it.
- At 16px and below, use the mark alone without the wordmark.

## Color

Calmponent is monochrome: black, white, and pure neutral grays. Color appears only for status: green for success, amber for warning, and red for errors. Every text color passes WCAG AA (4.5:1), and input borders and focus rings pass 3:1, checked in CI. See the [design system](/docs/design-system).

## Type

Geist for text and Geist Mono for code. Headings are semibold with slightly tight tracking (`-0.025em`); body text uses normal tracking.

## Motion

Every motion has a cause: something changed, so something moves, then it rests. One curve family (`ease-calm`, `ease-snap`, `ease-pop`, `ease-fade`) and reduced motion everywhere.

## Voice

Plain and specific. Short sentences, active voice, one idea at a time. Say what a component does, not how exciting it is. Errors say what failed and what to do next.
